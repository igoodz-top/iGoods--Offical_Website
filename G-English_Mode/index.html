Hello everyone, I'm iGood.

One-stop Digital Visual Solutions

iGood Studio specializes in website development, customized PPT design and short video creation, delivering one-stop digital visual solutions for individual creators and small teams. We focus on lightweight website construction, building blogs and community sites compatible with light/dark modes and cloud persistent storage. Our PPT designs balance minimalist business aesthetics and creative visuals for reports, presentations and exhibitions. We also provide full supporting short video services including cover design and editing. Adopting low-saturation premium green as our core visual tone, we prioritize both aesthetics and practicality. Whether personal blogs, commercial display pages, work presentation materials or promotional short videos, we tailor exclusive solutions to efficiently realize every digital creation with concise, smooth visual expression.
